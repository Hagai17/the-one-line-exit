An entry for the make.fun bounty **The One-Line Exit**
([bounty page](https://make.fun/bounties/u-kreutzx-the-one-line-exit-1dc652d1)):
write the funniest single-line resignation letter you can — one line,
1–140 characters, judged head-to-head by a comedy-editor AI.

## The entry

> Your Majesty, I resign as your food taster, effective immediately after lunch; if lunch goes well, consider this my two weeks' notice.

A royal food taster's resignation whose notice period is conditional on
surviving lunch. The drafting process — twelve candidates dueled against
the judge's published criteria (joke lands, surprise, form, specific
voice, quotability) — is recorded in [candidates.md](candidates.md).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Prints exactly the entry line and exits 0. Under the make.fun program
harness it also writes the same text to `/output/entry.txt`.
